02About

Planning and procurement for a multi-brand automotive parts distributor in Oman. Every order is tracked from enquiry until the goods are checked into the warehouse, and every decision can be explained.

03How I work

From enquiry to warehouse

  1. 01

    Plan

    Read real demand and decide what the range needs, and how much of it.

  2. 02

    Buy

    Compare quotes, negotiate on price and check every order line by line before it is signed.

  3. 03

    Ship

    Book the freight, follow it, and have the customs paperwork ready before the goods land.

  4. 04

    Receive

    Clear the goods, check them into the warehouse and match them back to the order.

04What I look after

Five things, done properly

  • 01

    Demand planning

    Forecasts built from real demand and reviewed every cycle, so every order can be explained.

  • 02

    Procurement

    Enquiries turned into purchase orders, negotiated on market rates and checked line by line before anyone signs.

  • 03

    Freight and customs

    Shipments booked and cleared start to finish, with the paperwork right the first time.

  • 04

    Controls and reporting

    Checks that catch expensive mistakes before money leaves, and reports leadership can act on.

  • 05

    Automation

    Repetitive work turned into systems anyone can run, so the process holds up when I am not there.
